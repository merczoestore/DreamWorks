02

Data We Collect

Personal Data You Provide

When You register for an account, We ask You to provide:

  • Full name
  • Email address

This information is used solely to create and manage Your account within the Service. Authentication and profile management are handled securely via Supabase, a third-party backend-as-a-service provider. Supabase processes Your data under strict data protection obligations in accordance with their own privacy policy.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. It may include information such as Your Device’s IP address, device type, operating system version, the features of the Service You interact with, timestamps, session duration, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

Purchase Data

DreamWorks is a paid application. All purchases are processed exclusively through Apple’s App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment card information. Billing is governed entirely by Apple’s own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

03

How We Use Your Data

We use the Personal Data We collect solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service, including monitoring usage to detect and resolve issues.
  • To manage Your Account — to allow You to register and access functionalities available to registered users.
  • To perform our contract — to honor the purchase You made through the App Store.
  • To contact You — to send essential notifications such as security updates or important changes to the Service. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails.
  • To manage Your requests — to respond to support enquiries You send to Us.
  • For service analytics — to understand how features are used and improve the Service experience.

04

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade Your Personal Data to any third party for commercial or marketing purposes — ever.

Limited Sharing

We share Your data only in the following strictly limited circumstances:

  • Supabase: Your name and email are stored and managed through Supabase to provide authentication and profile services. Supabase acts as a data processor under strict contractual data protection obligations.
  • Apple App Store: Purchase and device information is handled by Apple as part of App Store infrastructure.
  • Business transfers: If the Service is acquired or merged with another entity, Your Personal Data may be transferred. You will be notified before such a transfer takes place.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose Your data if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).

05

Data Retention

We retain Your Personal Data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

  • Account information (name, email): retained for the duration of Your account relationship, plus up to 24 months after account closure to handle post-termination issues.
  • Usage data: up to 24 months for feature analytics and service improvement.
  • Server logs (IP addresses, access times): up to 24 months for security monitoring and troubleshooting.

When retention periods expire, We securely delete or anonymize Your Personal Data. Anonymized data that cannot be linked back to You may be retained indefinitely for aggregate analytics purposes.

06

International Transfers

DreamWorks is available globally through the Apple App Store. Your information, including Personal Data, is processed at Our operating location in Wyoming, United States, and may be stored on servers operated by Supabase, which may be located outside of Your country of residence.

Where required by applicable law — including the GDPR for users in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom — We ensure that international transfers of Your Personal Data are subject to appropriate safeguards. No transfer will take place unless adequate controls are in place to protect the security of Your data.

Your Rights & Deletion

Your Rights

Depending on Your location, You may have the following rights regarding Your Personal Data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data We hold about You.
  • Correction: Request that inaccurate data be corrected.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of Your Personal Data.
  • Portability: Request Your data in a portable, machine-readable format (where applicable).
  • Objection / Restriction: Object to or restrict the processing of Your data (EEA/UK users — GDPR).
  • Non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against You for exercising Your rights (California users — CCPA/CPRA).

Deleting Your Data

You may request deletion of Your account and all associated Personal Data at any time by contacting Us at the email address listed in the Contact section below. We will process Your request within a reasonable timeframe. Please note that We may retain certain data where We have a legal obligation to do so.

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell Your Personal Data. California residents have the right to know what data is collected, to request deletion, and to be free from discrimination for exercising their privacy rights.

EEA & UK Residents (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If You are located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, You have the right to lodge a complaint with Your local data protection supervisory authority if You believe Your data has been processed unlawfully.

Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13 (or under 16 where required by applicable law, such as GDPR). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the applicable minimum age.

If We become aware that We have collected Personal Data from a child without verification of parental consent, We will take steps to remove that information from Our servers.
